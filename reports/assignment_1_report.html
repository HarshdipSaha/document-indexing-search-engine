Q2: Multiple indexing techniques

Stemmed keywords and word fragments.

The second index handles cases where the user only remembers part of a word. Typing compu should still find computer, computers and computing.

How the N-gram search works

  1. Store lowercase raw words with their document frequencies.
  2. Break the partial word into character N-grams.
  3. Intersect the N-gram lists to get candidate words.
  4. Check that the complete fragment exists in the candidate word.
  5. Aggregate word frequencies by document and rank the matches.
