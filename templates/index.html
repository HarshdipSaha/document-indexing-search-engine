How search works

Exact search removes stop words and applies Porter stemming. “Running” and “runs” both become “run.”

Partial search intersects character-gram postings and verifies substrings. “Compu” finds “computer.” Short fragments use smaller grams.

Document stop words are excluded. Partial query fragments are not stemmed or stop-word filtered. Only document bodies are indexed.
