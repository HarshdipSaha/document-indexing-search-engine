Inside the indexes

Exact: tokenize → remove stop words → stem → retrieve document frequencies. “Running” and “runs” both become “run.”

Partial: tokenize → remove stop words → intersect character-gram postings → verify the full fragment. “Compu” finds “computer” and “computing.” One- and two-character fragments use smaller grams. Query fragments are not removed as stop words. Stemming is deliberately skipped to preserve substrings.

Results are ranked by total query-term frequency, with OR matching. Repeated query terms count once. Titles are labels; only document bodies are indexed.
